Our final rules consisted of giving each card a specific role, listed below:
A: Wild
2: +2
3: -1
4: Add a line to the drawing in the center
5: Subtracts two cards from the back of the next player's hand
6: Subtracts two cards from the front of the next player's hand
7: Change seats once in the current direction
8: Everybody says "egg"; the last person to do so draws 2
9: Nothing
10: Nothing
J: Skip next person's turn
Q: Reverse direction
K: Wild
Joker: Wild +4

We started off with base "One" rules, only sleeving all the special cards along with a little marker to show what they are. Then, we added in a silly rule after the first round whilst we were still thinking of actually good rules (rubbing your belly and patting your head being required before declaring "One").  Then, we started with a standard swap of hands rule, swapping hands with turn flow.  This opened the game up to much more varied hands, seeing a lot more cards per game. Then, next game, we added in requiring everyone to say "Egg" when an eight was played, acting as a sort of "Not it" to encourage being attentive to the game.  Now that swappage was in play, we wanted to make it possible to modify other players hands more directly, introducing -1's as 3's and -2's as 5 and 6, with 5 taking from the back of their hand and 6 taking from the front, to make it so a player couldn't hoard their cards on either end to keep themself safe.   For a final rule, we thought it would be fun to have a community drawing, where you add a line when a 4 is played.  We ended up keeping 10's and 9's as nothing, however sleeving them anyway to make sure players would not be able to differentiate.  This left us with every player having a completely sleeved hand.

Why we chose what we chose:
We started by adding swaps as 7, this increased the amount of different hands a player would see in a game, as they were literally swapping seat/hands with the other players constantly. 
Now that players were moving amongst the hands, we considered how we could make players have more agency over other players hands as they switch, 
Which led us to adding in the -1 and -2's with  two types of minus two as to avoid having one side of the hand be used as a "dump side" for bad cards.
Then, we started adding in fun things like a "not it" type rule for 8, which makes players stay attentative to the game. 
Lastly, for 4, 9, and 10 we felt adding more things that directly impact play would make it too chaotic, so we made 4 be a community drawing where you add a line when you play a four, and made 9 and 10 be nothing yet sleeved as too make it so that way every single card is sleeved.
